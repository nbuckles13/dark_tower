MC must not keep a meeting that has no participants.

RULING (user, 2026-11-12): an MC meeting exists only while it has participants. It ends when the last participant leaves; a participant who joins afterwards gets a NEW meeting. Anything else is a memory leak.

EVIDENCE: MC creates the meeting actor when GC assigns the meeting ('Meeting actor created' at assignment), before any participant connects. A meeting that nobody connects to never sees a 'last participant leaves' event and is never ended. In the devloop cluster, mc_meetings_active=10 and mc_connections_active=0 more than 5h after the last env-test run. Loki shows exactly 10 meetings with 'Meeting actor created' plus a GC 'User joined meeting' and no further MC activity; for example meeting 20dfb97c-9c33-47d8-bf4d-d642f3d979ab at 2026-11-12T12:54:11Z. Earlier builds show the same non-zero floor between runs. Several env-tests join through GC without ever connecting to MC, so every Layer 7 run leaks meetings. Those meetings keep their MH assignments, key material and heartbeats indefinitely, and they get pulled into MH replacement during the resilience tests.

REQUIRED:
1. END ON A JOIN WINDOW. Keep creating the meeting actor at GC assignment, so every meeting MC has allocated counts in mc_meetings_active from creation; MC must never hold allocated meetings that its metrics do not show. A meeting that no participant connects to within a bounded join window ends through the SAME end path as an emptied meeting (MeetingEndCause::Empty in crates/mc-service/src/actors/messages.rs; the last-participant path in crates/mc-service/src/actors/meeting.rs). That path notifies GC, releases MHs, cleans up KEK and policy state, and decrements the metric. The window must be config-driven with a derived, justified default (e.g. from the join token's lifetime), not an invented constant. No meeting may outlive the window plus one tick with zero participants. Deferring actor creation to the first connect is NOT acceptable.
2. Keep the existing behaviour: a clean leave or close by the last participant ends the meeting immediately. The bounded abrupt-loss reconnect grace (MC_DISCONNECT_GRACE_PERIOD_SECONDS) stays. A join after the meeting ended must get a fresh meeting, including GC re-assignment if needed.
3. ADR-0010 §3 item 2 ('Meeting timeout: no participants for 1 hour, background job') and GC's crates/gc-service/src/tasks/assignment_cleanup.rs predate this ruling. Amend ADR-0010 §3 so it states the MC-side rule, and state what GC's job is now: a backstop for rows MC never reported, or removed. Do not invent mechanism beyond what the ruling requires.
4. Tests: mc-service unit/integration tests for a never-connected meeting ending within the window (GC notified, handlers released), a join just after the end creating a new meeting, and the immediate end on a clean last leave. ONE env-test that proves it on the cluster using evidence scoped to its own meeting ID (e.g. Loki: its meeting_id ends with cause empty within window + bound). Do NOT assert on global counters such as mc_meetings_active, and do NOT wait for global state to settle. The env-test may add at most about window + 10s to Layer 7; report its measured runtime in main.md.
5. Observability: document the window config, its gauge (follow the existing mc_* config-gauge pattern), and the end cause for a window expiry in docs/observability/metrics/mc-service.md. A meeting ended by the join window must be distinguishable from one emptied by a leave, in logs and in whatever end-cause metric MC already has.
