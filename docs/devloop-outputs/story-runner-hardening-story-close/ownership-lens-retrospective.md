## Pattern Observations
- **Not templated.** All four tables are per-file with specific reasoning, and two corrected their own rows at Gate 3 when the shipped diff outgrew the planned row (`env.ts`/`fixtures.ts` in `2026-08-15`; the `run-story.test.sh` upgrade in `2026-08-17`). Under-recording, not boilerplate, is this story's failure mode.
- **Same edit shape, two tiers — live drift.** The one-line `scripts/layer3.sh` `run_and_emit` wiring line was upgraded Mechanical→Minor-judgment in `2026-08-13` (U-1, decided on §6.2's requirement that Mechanical be guard-pipeline-covered), filed Minor-judgment in `2026-08-15`, then filed **Mechanical** in `2026-08-17` with no reviewer challenge. U-1 turned on guard coverage of the file × change-pattern, which the implementer's owner-ness does not change.
- **GSA-by-criterion is being applied correctly and caught a would-be Mechanical.** `2026-08-17` declared `crates/dt-story/**` criterion-matched under §6.4 (schema evolution, path-independent) and upgraded four fixture rows off Mechanical on that basis. `2026-08-14` and `2026-08-15` both independently noted the same criterion-over-glob reading for `migrations/**`. No GSA path was routed as Mechanical in any of the four.
- **§6.5 pairing is the only Domain-judgment remedy used: 4 of 4 devloops, 0 owner-implemented spin-outs.** `2026-08-15` records the mechanism — under headless `run-story`, §6.3's owner-implements route terminates the story — and dropped five Design-A rows at Gate 1 rather than downgrade them. That is a systemic pressure toward pairing which ADR-0024 §6.3 does not acknowledge.
- **Pattern B did not occur.** No coordinated convention-driven rename in any devloop, so the named-convention-author requirement (§6.6 step 4) was never exercised and cannot be assessed from this story.
- **Trailer practice split 2/2, and one stated trailer never landed.** `09a5499` and `9d3b94b` carry `Approved-Cross-Boundary:` trailers; `33ace8d` and `d096de0` carry none — though `2026-08-13`'s main.md states the `ci.yml` trailer offered by @paired-infrastructure was taken. `9d3b94b`'s two trailers initially sat in their own paragraph and returned nothing from `git interpret-trailers --parse`, amended at `73fb511`. Root cause is textual: `review-protocol.md` §Ownership Lens asks, for Minor-judgment, "has the owner recorded an `Approved-Cross-Boundary:` trailer for each hunk they own?" while ADR-0024 §6.6/§6.7 makes the trailer optional and Gate-1/Gate-3 confirmation the mechanism.
- **Zero formal ESCALATED verdicts** despite §6.2's auto-route firing ten times; resolved each time by the `2026-08-13` Lead arbitration (an uncontested Gate-1 upgrade does not force ESCALATED). Already filed under `docs/TODO.md` §From ADR-0024 §6 Amendment — this story is the fourth and fifth exercise of a rule that must be re-arbitrated whenever it is used.
